Project Overview

Legit Insights is a sophisticated Legal Tech solution sitting at the pivotal intersection of jurisprudence and generative artificial intelligence. It transforms how legal professionals interact with massive case volumes.

The platform addresses the critical bottleneck of legal document review, providing instant semantic insights that go beyond simple keyword matching, ensuring that lawyers find exactly what they need in record time.

The Blueprints

System Architecture

Ingestion Layer

Raw legal data is processed through our NLP pipeline, identifying entities, clauses, and precedents with 92% accuracy.

Cognitive Core

The BART-driven engine handles the 'reasoning' phase, connecting identified legal facts into a coherent summary.

Vector Search

Pinecone serves as our long-term memory, enabling Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for factual precision.

hub Neural Processing

Our NLP engine uses a sophisticated neural network to map legal relationships. By visualizing the "hidden" layers of decision making, we ensure transparency in how AI interprets case law.

data_object Knowledge Graph

We transform static PDF documents into a dynamic Knowledge Graph. This allows for predictive modeling where the system can suggest potential legal outcomes based on historic precedents.
