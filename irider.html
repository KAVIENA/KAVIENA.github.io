Architecting real-time vision systems for e-scooter rider evaluation through LiDAR and sensor fusion.

The Challenge

As urban mobility shifts towards micro-vehicles, rider safety becomes a data-driven imperative. We faced the complex task of designing a collaborative research framework to analyze rider safety through high-frequency sensor data and LiDAR environmental mapping, bridging the gap between raw physics and digital intelligence.
